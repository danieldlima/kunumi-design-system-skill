# Kunumi Profile-Photo System

Read this reference for AI-assisted portraits, profile photos, avatars, or the Kunumi IA profile-photo asset.

Source authority:

- `../assets/local/channels/profile/marca.png`
- The Kunumi IA profile-photo guide is not bundled. Ask the user for it when the task needs a
  ruling this reference does not already record.

## Input Photo

Use a recent, sharp, color photo with:

- the face clearly visible and in focus;
- simple, neutral surroundings;
- a square crop when possible;
- bust-to-head framing;
- no dark glasses, hats, heavy shadows, social-media filters, extreme angles, distortion, other people, or low resolution.

## Generated Portrait

Preserve the person's facial identity and natural skin appearance. Create:

- a square 1:1 photorealistic, high-resolution studio portrait;
- a neck-or-bust-up crop;
- a mostly forward-facing pose with a slight natural head tilt;
- soft, multidimensional natural light without harsh shadows;
- a near-solid background with a very subtle infinite-studio gradient;
- a plain crew-neck shirt;
- the supplied mark fully visible and centered on the chest;
- shallow depth of field with an 85 mm portrait-lens feel.

Use the supplied `marca.png`; do not redraw or approximate the icon.

## Review Gate

Before delivery, verify:

- likeness and facial proportions remain faithful to the source;
- skin texture and lighting look natural;
- the result is square and high resolution;
- the selected colors match the exact table values;
- the shirt is plain and crew-neck;
- the supplied mark is complete, centered, correctly colored, and not distorted;
- the background remains nearly solid and does not distract from the face;
- no unwanted accessories, extra people, text, or generated logo artifacts appear.

If repeated generations introduce distortions, restart from the original photo and approved mark rather than compounding edits.
