# Kunumi Visual Behavior

Authority: **Brandbook Kunumi Final (2025)**, capítulos *comportamento visual* and *introdução*.

## Brand Architecture

Three marks, each with a defined audience. Quoted from the architecture page.

### Kunumi (core)

> "Utilizada para falar com todos os Kunumis e stakeholders específicos."

- **Público:** interno. Ocasionalmente, conselho do banco.
- **Uso:** comunicações gerais com colaboradores e comunicações com o conselho para apresentação
  da visão geral do ecossistema.
- **Exemplos:** apresentações, reuniões, ações e eventos com colaboradores, câmbio, kit de
  boas-vindas Kunumi, entre outros.

### Kunumi Unlimited

> "Marca que representa a empresa LTDA e o time de aplicação de tecnologias no Bradesco."

- **Público:** externo: Bradesco. Interno: assuntos relacionados ao Bradesco.
- **Uso:** comunicações e iniciativas realizadas com o Bradesco.
- **Exemplos:** apresentações, ações, eventos e reuniões com o Bradesco, papelaria, dashboards,
  relatórios, entre outros.

### Instituto Kunumi

> "Marca que representa as diversas iniciativas de atuação do Instituto Kunumi."

- **Público:** externo. Interno: iniciativas do Instituto.
- **Uso:** comunicações e iniciativas do Instituto.
- **Exemplos:** apresentações e eventos do universo acadêmico, além de materiais relacionados a
  conteúdo e pesquisa.

Do not mix lockups or expressive systems across these three unless the user explicitly asks for a
co-branded treatment. Then follow the brand-row rules in `logo-governance.md`.

### Documented gap — Kunumi Colab

**Kunumi Colab** appears in the verbal identity as a correct brand name, but the architecture page
carries only the three marks above, and no Colab mark, palette, or usage rule is documented in the
chapters read. Treat Colab as a **name only**. Ask for approved material before designing anything
Colab-branded; do not borrow another identity's system for it.

## The "Versus" Symbol

> "O ícone, batizado de Versus, simboliza ao mesmo tempo ruptura e homenagem aos ícones
> anteriores. Embora faça referência clara à letra K, ele remete a um X, que reflete uma oposição
> dinâmica e funciona como uma janela para os variados universos da organização."

The symbol is the **pivô central** from which the Instituto Kunumi and Kunumi Unlimited marks are
built. It is not decoration and not a pattern tile — use the supplied mark files.

Supporting texture: the descriptors use **hachuras** (hatching) that reference the texture of
digital screens and glitches. New colors and gradients widen the visual range. Use the hatching as
supplied inside approved artwork; do not synthesize a hatch pattern to imitate it.

Origin of the name, for context in institutional copy: **Kunumi means "criança" in Tupi-Guarani.**

## Print Formats

The brandbook defines its own **non-ISO** "Kunumi" formats. These differ from real ISO sizes —
ISO A4 is 210×297 mm, ISO A3 is 297×420 mm. Use the Kunumi values when a brandbook format is
named, and never silently substitute the ISO equivalent.

| Format | Size | MDC (margem) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Postal | 100 × 150 mm | **10 mm** |
| A5 Kunumi | 150 × 210 mm | **30 mm** |
| A4 Kunumi | 210 × 300 mm | **30 mm** |
| A3 Kunumi | 300 × 450 mm | **60 mm** |
| A2 Kunumi | 420 × 600 mm | **60 mm** |

MDC is the minimum clear margin on the piece. It is independent of, and additional to, the logo's
own 1X clear space.

> **Scope note.** Print production is outside this skill's delivery scope. These values are
> recorded as governance facts so that color, margin, and logo advice stays correct when a print
> piece is discussed, specified, or reviewed. For a print build, ask for the approved source file.

Print color: use the CMYK and Pantone values in `tokens.json`, never a screen hex converted by
eye.

## Composition

- Strong left-aligned hierarchy on a Gelo ground, generous empty space.
- Two-by-two grid: two major horizontal blocks, two major vertical blocks, then subdivide.
- Hairline rules and precise edge alignment carry the structure. Rounded-card UI language is not
  the default; the brandbook's own cards use a **10 px** radius when a card is genuinely needed.
- One narrative job per page or slide, one dominant message, one reading order.
- Urucum is a reading accent, used selectively — not an all-over fill.
- The mark sits small in page furniture; it appears large only on covers, identity stories, and
  resource pages.

The canonical text block is a **360 px** column: uppercase display title, 60 px gap, Figtree Light
body. See `typography.md`.

## Gate

- Right mark for the right audience; no cross-identity mixing.
- Kunumi format values used when a brandbook format is named.
- MDC respected, and separate from logo clear space.
- Hatching, gradients, and the Versus symbol used as supplied, never redrawn.
- Nothing invented for Kunumi Colab.
